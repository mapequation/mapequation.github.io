[Network Navigator](https://mapequation.org/navigator/) is rebuilt on
[d3gl](https://mapequation.org/d3gl/) and now shows raw or clustered networks
with millions of links in the browser. Open an Infomap `.ftree` result, or load
a raw network and cluster it with
[Infomap](https://mapequation.org/infomap/) without leaving the page. Choose a
plain or nested layout. The level of detail, based on the module hierarchy or on
spatial proximity, expands continuously as you zoom in. State networks can be
shown with physical nodes, state nodes, or both. Export the view as SVG or PNG,
or the result as `.ftree`
([GitHub](https://github.com/mapequation/navigator)).
