[d3gl](https://mapequation.org/d3gl/) draws d3 maps, plots, and node–link
networks on SVG, Canvas2D, or WebGL2 through one API. It renders millions of
data points in real time and adapts the level of detail as you zoom.
Context-driven d3 generators such as `geoPath` and `d3-shape` render on the GPU
unchanged. d3gl powers
[Infomap Bioregions](https://mapequation.org/bioregions/) and
[Network Navigator](https://mapequation.org/navigator/). Install it with
`npm install @mapequation/d3gl`
([docs and live examples](https://mapequation.org/d3gl/),
[GitHub](https://github.com/mapequation/d3gl)).
